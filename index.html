About Me

My name is Devin Moorjani and I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of California, Riverside. This portfolio showcases some of the engineering projects I've created.

SolidWorks Projects

V6 Engine

The first intern project I did for FSAE at UCR. I created a large assembly with 20+ parts and learned how to use gear/mechanical mates in SolidWorks. I designed an exploded view drawing with a bill of materials.

Marble Run

With this project, I created a basic motion study with contact forces and gravity. I utilized gears from the toolbox and experimented with different speeds for the marble.

Green Lantern Battery & Ring

I designed 5 custom parts; 3 of them with lip and groove fits and 0.05 mm tolerances. I plan to eventually 3D print it and put green LEDs inside.
